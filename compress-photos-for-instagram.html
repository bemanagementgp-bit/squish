    Compress Photos for Instagram Without Losing Quality: how it works

    Instagram re-encodes everything you upload. Hand it a 20 MB RAW export at 6000 px and its server will downscale and compress aggressively, which is where the muddy, blocky results come from. Hand it an image that is already at the right size and the re-encode barely touches it.

    Squish lets you do that preparation in the browser for free: resize to 1080 px, set a high JPEG quality and download, with no upload to any third party.

    Instagram's real dimensions

    Instagram displays feed images at 1080 px wide and stores nothing wider. The aspect ratio determines the height:

    • Square: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1)
    • Portrait: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5), the tallest a feed post can be
    • Landscape: 1080 × 566 px (1.91:1)
    • Stories and Reels covers: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)

    In Squish, choose the 1080 px (Instagram) preset under Max dimension. The longest side becomes 1080 px and the other side scales proportionally, so crop to the ratio you want first in your phone's editor.

    Quality setting and color

    Because Instagram will re-compress once more, you want to leave headroom. Export as JPEG at quality 85–90 rather than the 75 that works for websites. At 1080 px that yields files of 250–500 KB, well under the roughly 1.6 MB point where Instagram's recompression becomes noticeably more aggressive.

    Keep the color space sRGB; wide-gamut exports from Lightroom look dull after upload. Squish's canvas encoder writes standard sRGB JPEGs and strips EXIF data, so location metadata does not travel with the post. Avoid PNG for photos, as Instagram converts it to JPEG anyway and the larger upload just takes longer.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why do my Instagram photos look blurry after upload?

    Usually because the upload was far larger than 1080 px and Instagram downscaled it with heavy compression. Resizing to 1080 px yourself and exporting at quality 85–90 avoids most of the damage.

    What is the best file size for an Instagram post?

    Between 200 KB and 1 MB at 1080 px wide. Anything larger gains nothing because Instagram stores a 1080 px version regardless.

    Can I prepare a whole carousel at once?

    Yes. Drop all ten slides into Squish, set the preset once and download them together. The free plan covers exactly 10 images per batch, matching a carousel.

    Does Squish keep my photos private?

    Yes. All processing happens locally in the browser; nothing is uploaded, logged or stored.
