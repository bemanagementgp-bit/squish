    Compress Images for Shopify: how it works

    Product photography is the heaviest asset on any store. Twelve photos per product at 6 MB each turn a collection page into a 70 MB download before the CDN steps in, and slow collection pages cost conversions on mobile.

    Squish lets you resize and compress a product shoot in the browser before uploading it to Shopify. It is free and private: files are processed on your computer and never uploaded to any third party.

    Shopify's image requirements

    The numbers to design around:

    • Maximum upload: 20 MB per image and 20 megapixels (4472 × 4472 px).
    • Recommended product image: 2048 × 2048 px square. This is large enough for the theme zoom feature and small enough to load quickly.
    • Collection and hero banners: usually 1920–2560 px wide depending on the theme.
    • Formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP are accepted; Shopify's CDN serves WebP automatically to browsers that support it.

    In Squish, set Max dimension to 2048 px (choose 2560 and let the theme crop if your photos are not square) and keep the output as JPEG at quality 80. A typical product shot lands at 250–500 KB.

    Consistency across a catalog

    Themes look best when every image in a grid shares the same aspect ratio and background tone. Crop to square in your editor, then run the whole batch through Squish with identical settings so every file gets the same dimensions and compression level. The tool processes images in parallel and reports the total space saved.

    Keep PNG only for images with transparent backgrounds used on colored sections; for white-background product photos, JPEG is 5–10× smaller. Squish removes camera EXIF data as part of the re-encode, so lens and location metadata does not end up on a public CDN.

    Frequently asked questions

    What image size does Shopify recommend for products?

    2048 × 2048 pixels, square. Files must be under 20 MB and 20 megapixels, but for speed aim for under 500 KB each.

    Does Shopify compress images automatically?

    Shopify's CDN resizes and converts to WebP on delivery, but it starts from the original you upload. Uploading a well-compressed 2048 px file gives better results and faster theme previews than uploading a 6 MB camera file.

    Can I use this for Etsy or WooCommerce too?

    Yes. Etsy recommends at least 2000 px on the shortest side and WooCommerce works well with 1200–2048 px squares; the same Squish workflow applies, only the max dimension changes.

    How many product photos can I compress at once?

    The free plan handles 10 images per batch, up to 10 MB each. Pro removes the batch limit and allows 50 MB files for high-resolution shoots.
